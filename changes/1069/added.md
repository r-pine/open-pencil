- Keep documents on OpenPencil Cloud or your team's self-hosted server and edit them together. Connect a server in **Settings → OpenPencil Cloud**; the web app signs in on the server's pages, and the desktop app or another editor address approves a code in the browser. Home lists the server's workspaces and the documents shared with you, **File → Save to Cloud…** saves the open document to a workspace, and the status beside the name says whether it is saved, uploading, waiting offline, or changed in two places, with the choice to keep both versions. Opening a Cloud document joins its live room, so everyone with it open sees edits and cursors as they happen while one editor saves for all. **Share** invites people by email and turns on a link that opens the document for guests, within what the workspace's plan allows. The server, its relay, and its pages for signing in, account security, and administration are in `@open-pencil/cloud`.
